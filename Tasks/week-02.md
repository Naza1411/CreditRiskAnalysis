# Week 2 — Build a Repayment Schedule

## Assignment to send

Cedar MFB needs an auditable Excel calculator for flat-rate and reducing-balance loans. Build it and explain why the quoted rate alone may not describe the customer's true borrowing cost.

### Learn — maximum 2 hours

Study principal, interest, fees, tenor, instalment, outstanding principal, flat-rate interest, reducing-balance interest and effective borrowing cost.

### Build — 6 hours

1. Create an input sheet for principal, annual rate, fees, disbursement date, frequency and term.
2. Generate separate flat-rate and reducing-balance schedules.
3. Show due date, opening balance, principal, interest, fee, total instalment and closing balance.
4. Add input validation and highlight invalid or missing entries.
5. Compare three scenarios: ₦50,000 for 3 months, ₦150,000 for 6 months and ₦500,000 for 12 months.
6. Write a short interpretation of the total amount paid and affordability implications.

### Submit

- `repayment_schedule_calculator.xlsx`
- Screenshot or PDF of the three scenario outputs
- A 200-word business note comparing the methods

### Quality checks

- Principal components sum to the original principal.
- The last closing balance is zero, subject to documented rounding.
- Dates and instalment counts match the term.
- Inputs are separate from formulas.

### Stretch task

Add an early-settlement scenario and document the assumed treatment of fees and accrued interest.
